## Line of business (from the live pages)

B&G Machine and Welding, 3801 Gilmore Ave, Bakersfield, CA 93308, is a full-service machine shop and welding shop. The home page states manual lathes, Bridgeport-style mills, one CNC mill, MIG / TIG / HELI-ARC / STICK welding, and CNC flame and plasma cutting on a 6 ft x 12 ft bed. Work is listed for oilfield, farming, mining, wind energy, manufacturing, food production, transportation, and maintenance. About Us says the shop was founded in 1951 by brothers Bud & Gordon McCain, and that B&G manufactures and installs the Hobble-Clamp safety system and services and installs Annugas Production Enhancers. A 24-hour emergency call-out line is on the home page and points at Contact. Phone on every page is 661-322-7764. About Us and Contact also list 661-322-8621 with no label, and 661-322-9004 as fax. Published email on About Us and Contact is contact@bgmach.com. The footer `mailto:email@adress.com` is the PhotonFX placeholder and is not used on this demo.

## Peer sites (layout ideas only)

### 1. Industrial Machine Company — https://industrialmachinecompany.com/

Full-service machine shop with machining, welding, and 24/7 field work for oil and gas. Ideas borrowed:

- Put a short emergency call strip directly under the hero, because their page leads with "available 24/7 when it matters most." Here that strip uses B&G's own lines "Call Today : 661-322-7764" and "24 Hour Emergency Call Out is Available."
- Follow the hero with three service blocks (they use machining, welding and fabrication, and a third specialty). Here the three blocks are B&G Machine, B&G Welding, and B&G Cutting, in that order, with the original sentences.
- Close with a plain "need a part" contact prompt. The demo's pre-footer band is B&G's phone and address only.

### 2. Martin Machine & Welding — https://martinmachineandwelding.com/

Machine shop and welding company that shows oil and gas and agriculture among the industries it serves, plus a work gallery. Ideas borrowed:

- Industries as a compact chip row instead of a tall stack of oversized lines. Chips use only B&G's eight industry names.
- A captioned photo gallery as its own view, with a calm lightbox, matching their "Gallery of our work" placement. Captions are the twelve labels from B&G's Projects page.
- Each capability sits beside a shop photograph rather than inside a large padded card.

### 3. Keystone Industries — https://kstoneindustries.com/

Precision machine shop that lists manual machining, CNC, plasma cutting, and MIG/TIG welding as separate core services. Ideas borrowed:

- Uncovered headline under the photo: a plain statement of what the shop does, then the call action, then the service list. Their hero is a short promise plus "Request a Quote"; this demo keeps the quote off the photo and uses B&G's call line.
- Specs as a scannable chip list under each service (they separate manual machining, CNC, cutting, and welding). Chips repeat only figures already in B&G's sentences (lathe sizes, bed size, flame and plasma thicknesses).
- A house product pulled out of the general service list (they split Agitation Resources into its own block). Hobble-Clamp gets its own page, because the live nav already links to hobble-clamp.com, plus a short teaser on the home page using that page's sentences.

No peer text, photos, or branding is copied.
