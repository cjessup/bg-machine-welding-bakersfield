Founded in 1951 by brothers Bud & Gordon McCain. B&G Machine and Welding has always tried to provide a customer "first aproach" to the machine and welding trade.

Whether you have a small job or large project let B&G Machine and Welding take care of your machine, welding, and cutting needs.
