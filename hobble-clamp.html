Hobble Clamp Safety System

In a safety-conscious oilfield, the Hobble-Clamp safety tie-down is a must for your work-over safety needs. In the past, variations of the hold-down equipment used, ranged from chains or slings tossed over the walking beam - to multiple types of unproven hooks and clamps not engineered for controling pumping unit movement. The Hobble-Clamp is the proven positive lock-out system to control stored energy on a pumping unit while servicing a well.

Engineered to be a proven tool, rated to withstand the weight of your pumping unit with a 3-to-1 safety factor built-in. The Hobble-Clamp has not only been pull-tested to destruction to prove its strength and integrity, but is proven daily in safety conscious fields.
